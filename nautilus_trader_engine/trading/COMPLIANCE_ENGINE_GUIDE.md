# Compliance Engine Guide

## Overview

The Compliance Engine is a comprehensive, real-time compliance monitoring and enforcement system designed for institutional trading environments. It provides pre-trade compliance checking, position limit monitoring, configurable regulatory rule engines, and comprehensive compliance reporting and audit trail capabilities.

## Key Features

### ✅ **Pre-Trade Compliance Checking**
- **Real-Time Validation**: Sub-millisecond pre-trade compliance validation
- **Multi-Level Checks**: Account, portfolio, and firm-level compliance validation
- **Risk Limit Enforcement**: Comprehensive risk limit checking and enforcement
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Built-in regulatory rule compliance (MiFID II, Reg NMS, etc.)

### 📊 **Position Limit Monitoring**
- **Real-Time Position Tracking**: Continuous monitoring of position limits across all accounts
- **Multi-Asset Limit Management**: Position limits for equities, options, futures, and other instruments
- **Concentration Risk Monitoring**: Sector, industry, and single-name concentration limits
- **Dynamic Limit Adjustment**: Automated limit adjustments based on market conditions

### ⚖️ **Regulatory Rule Engine**
- **Configurable Rules**: Flexible rule engine supporting custom compliance requirements
- **Multi-Jurisdiction Support**: Support for global regulatory requirements
- **Rule Versioning**: Version control and audit trail for rule changes
- **Exception Management**: Comprehensive exception handling and escalation procedures

### 📋 **Compliance Reporting & Audit Trails**
- **Real-Time Reporting**: Live compliance dashboards and monitoring
- **Regulatory Reporting**: Automated generation of regulatory reports
- **Audit Trail Management**: Comprehensive audit trails for all trading activities
- **Exception Reporting**: Detailed reporting of compliance violations and exceptions
