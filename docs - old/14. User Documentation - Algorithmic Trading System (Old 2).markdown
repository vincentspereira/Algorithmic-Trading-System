### **2.0 Getting Started**

Let's get you set up and ready to trade.

#### **2.1 Creating Your Account**

1. Navigate to the Nautilus Trader homepage and click "Sign Up."
2. Enter your details to create your account.
3. You will receive a verification email. Click the link in the email to confirm your account.

#### **2.2 Your First Login & Securing Your Account**

Security is our top priority. Upon your first login, you will be prompted to set up **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**.

1. Log in with your new credentials.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions to link your account with an authenticator app (like Google Authenticator or Authy) on your smartphone.
3. Scan the QR code and enter the 6-digit code from your app to complete the setup.

From now on, you will need both your password and a code from your authenticator app to log in, providing a critical layer of security for your account.

#### **2.3 Connecting a Brokerage Account**

Nautilus Trader is a platform for managing your strategies; your funds and positions remain securely with your broker. You must connect a brokerage account to enable trading.

1. Navigate to the **"Settings"** page from the main dashboard.
2. Go to the **"Broker Integrations"** tab.
3. Click **"Add New Broker"** and select your broker from the list (e.g., **Interactive Brokers**, **Alpaca**).
4. Follow the secure on-screen instructions to enter your broker's API keys. These keys create a secure link allowing Nautilus Trader to manage trades on your behalf.
   * **Note:** Your credentials are encrypted and stored securely using enterprise-grade secret management.

### **4.0 The Three Ways to Trade: Find Your Perfect Workflow**

Nautilus Trader is designed to adapt to you. We offer three distinct, powerful ways to develop, test, and deploy your trading strategies.

#### **4.1 For the Everyday Investor: The No-Code Strategy Builder** 🎨

Want to automate your trading ideas without writing any code? The No-Code Builder is for you. It uses a simple, visual drag-and-drop interface powered by **Blockly**.

**Tutorial: Creating a Simple Moving Average Crossover Strategy**

1. **Open the Builder:** From the main menu, navigate to **"Strategies" -\> "No-Code Builder."**
2. **Create a New Strategy:** Click "Create New" and give your strategy a name, like "My First Crossover."
3. **Define Your Trigger:**
   * From the "Market Data" blocks, drag a `When...` block onto the canvas.
   * From the "Indicators" blocks, drag a `Moving Average` block and drop it into the `When...` block. Set its period to `10`.
   * From the "Logic" blocks, drag a `crosses above` block.
   * Drag another `Moving Average` block, set its period to `50`, and connect it.
   * Your trigger should now read: **"When MA(10) crosses above MA(50) for symbol AAPL..."**
4. **Define Your Action:**
   * From the "Actions" blocks, drag a `Place Market Order` block and connect it inside the `When...` block.
   * Set the parameters: `Side: BUY`, `Quantity: 100`.
5. **Backtest It:** Click the **"Backtest"** button. Select a date range and let the engine run your strategy against historical data. You'll get a detailed report with key metrics like **Sharpe Ratio** and **Maximum Drawdown** to see how it would have performed.
6. **Deploy It:** Happy with the results? Click **"Deploy"** and assign the strategy to your paper or live trading account. It's that simple\!

#### **4.3 For the Professional Quant: The Python Studio** 💻

The Python Studio is a full-featured, browser-based development environment for professional quantitative analysts who need maximum power and flexibility.

**Key Features:**

* **NautilusTrader Engine:** Write your strategies in Python using the high-performance, institutional-grade NautilusTrader framework.
* **AI Co-pilot (OpenHands):** You're never coding alone. The studio has an integrated AI assistant specifically trained to help with Python code.
  * Stuck on a bug? Just ask: `_"@OpenHands, I'm getting a KeyError on line 42, can you help me debug?"_`
  * Need to improve performance? `_"@OpenHands, can you refactor this function to be more efficient?"_`
* **Full Lifecycle Management:** Write, debug, backtest, and deploy your most complex strategies directly from the studio.

**Example Basic Strategy:**

```python
# A simple strategy to buy when the price crosses above a moving average
from nautilus_trader.model.strategy import Strategy
from nautilus_trader.model.data import Bar
from nautilus_trader.indicator import MovingAverage

class MySimpleStrategy(Strategy):
    def on_start(self):
        self.ma = MovingAverage(period=50)
        self.add_indicator(self.ma)

    def on_bar(self, bar: Bar):
        if not self.ma.is_ready:
            return

        if bar.close > self.ma.value:
            # Logic to submit a buy order
            self.log.info("Price is above MA, considering a buy.")
```

### **7.0 Glossary**

* **Agentic AI:** An AI system made of multiple, specialized AI "agents" that collaborate to solve complex problems.
* **Backtesting:** The process of testing a trading strategy on historical data to see how it would have performed.
* **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** An AI technique that allows the model to "look up" information from provided documents before answering a question, leading to more accurate and factual responses.
* **Sharpe Ratio:** A measure of risk-adjusted return. A higher Sharpe Ratio is generally better.
* **VaR (Value at Risk):** A statistic that quantifies the level of financial risk within a portfolio over a specific time frame.
