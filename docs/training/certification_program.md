# Algorithmic Trading System Certification Program

## Program Overview

The Algorithmic Trading System Certification Program is a comprehensive, competency-based certification framework designed to validate expertise in algorithmic trading system development, deployment, and management. The program offers multiple certification levels to accommodate different skill levels and career paths.

## Certification Levels

### Level 1: Platform Fundamentals Certification
**Target Audience:** Beginners, traders new to algorithmic trading  
**Duration:** 40 hours of study + assessment  
**Prerequisites:** None  
**Validity:** 2 years

### Level 2: Advanced Strategy Development Certification
**Target Audience:** Intermediate users, quantitative analysts  
**Duration:** 80 hours of study + assessment  
**Prerequisites:** Level 1 certification or equivalent experience  
**Validity:** 2 years

### Level 3: Professional Trading Systems Certification
**Target Audience:** Advanced practitioners, system architects  
**Duration:** 120 hours of study + assessment  
**Prerequisites:** Level 2 certification + 1 year experience  
**Validity:** 3 years

### Specialist Certifications
- **Risk Management Specialist**
- **API Integration Specialist**
- **Machine Learning for Trading Specialist**
- **Multi-Asset Trading Specialist**

### Assessment Structure

#### Knowledge Assessment (40%)
**Format:** Online multiple-choice and scenario-based questions  
**Duration:** 2 hours  
**Passing Score:** 80%

**Sample Questions:**

1. **Multiple Choice (Basic Concepts)**
   ```
   Which of the following best describes a momentum trading strategy?
   
   A) Buying assets that have declined in price
   B) Buying assets that are showing upward price movement
   C) Buying assets at random intervals
   D) Buying assets based on fundamental analysis only
   
   Answer: B
   Explanation: Momentum strategies capitalize on the continuation of existing price trends.
   ```

2. **Scenario-Based (Risk Management)**
   ```
   You have a $100,000 portfolio and want to limit risk per trade to 2%. 
   Your strategy has a stop-loss of 5%. What is the maximum position size 
   you should take?
   
   A) $20,000
   B) $40,000
   C) $50,000
   D) $100,000
   
   Answer: B
   Calculation: Risk per trade ($2,000) ÷ Stop-loss percentage (5%) = $40,000
   ```

#### Practical Assessment (60%)
**Format:** Hands-on platform exercises  
**Duration:** 4 hours  
**Passing Score:** 75%

**Assessment Tasks:**

**Task 1: Strategy Creation (25%)**
```
Create a moving average crossover strategy with the following specifications:
- Use 10-day and 30-day simple moving averages
- Buy when short MA crosses above long MA
- Sell when short MA crosses below long MA
- Include 3% stop-loss and 6% take-profit
- Set position size to 5% of portfolio
- Add volume filter (volume > 20-day average)

Deliverables:
- Completed strategy configuration
- Strategy documentation (500 words)
- Logic validation screenshots
```

**Task 2: Backtesting and Analysis (25%)**
```
Backtest your created strategy with these parameters:
- Date range: January 1, 2020 to December 31, 2023
- Initial capital: $100,000
- Test universe: S&P 500 stocks (top 50 by market cap)
- Benchmark: SPY ETF
- Include transaction costs: 0.1% per trade

Deliverables:
- Backtest results summary
- Performance metrics analysis
- Comparison with benchmark
- Strategy strengths and weaknesses report (750 words)
```

**Task 3: Risk Management Implementation (10%)**
```
Enhance your strategy with advanced risk management:
- Maximum 3 concurrent positions
- Daily loss limit of 1%
- Maximum correlation of 0.7 between positions
- Position sizing based on volatility

Deliverables:
- Updated strategy configuration
- Risk management documentation
- Validation of risk controls
```
